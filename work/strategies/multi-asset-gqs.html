01The idea

This module runs four allocation ideas at once: follow the trend, fade the extreme, respect the macro backdrop and balance the risk. They disagree often, which is the point.

02The signal

Four components:

  • Momentum, 30%. Twelve-minus-one-month return, against the asset's own past and against the others.
  • Mean reversion, 25%. Five-day moves against a 60-day norm, and five relative-value pairs.
  • Macro, 25%. Industrial production, real yields, financial conditions and the dollar, through a fixed tilt table.
  • Risk parity, 20%. A base that equalises each asset's risk.

03Sizing and risk

10% volatility target, at most 2.5× leverage, 20% per position. Rebalanced daily, with a five-day minimum hold and a 30% daily turnover cap. Weights smoothed to hold down trading.

04Results on record

The validation report gives a Sharpe ratio of 0.27 from 2010, 0.36 out of sample and 0.26 walk-forward, on a 1.4% annual return at 5.7% volatility. No return series was saved for this strategy, so the figures are quoted from the run records.

05What works, and what does not

  • Modest: a Sharpe ratio near 0.3, most of it appearing only after turnover was capped.
  • In-sample step: the regime adjustment uses the latest growth and inflation reading for the whole test.
  • Missing inputs: two sentiment series in the macro component do not load.
