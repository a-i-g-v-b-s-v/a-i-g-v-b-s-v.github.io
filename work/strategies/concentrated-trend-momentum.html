Concentrated trend and momentum

At most four funds at a time, each in a confirmed uptrend, with a fixed defensive portfolio for bad markets.

The fourth version drops the breadth of the earlier ones. It holds only the top four of 15 funds, requires each to be trending and positive over a year, and has three explicit states: risk-on, cautious and risk-off.

01The idea

Diversifying across twenty funds dilutes the few that are really moving. This version concentrates, and pays for the extra risk with a stricter entry test and a clear plan for when nothing passes it.

02The signal

A fund must have a positive trend score and a positive twelve-month return to be held at all. Among those that pass:

  • Momentum, 60%. Returns over 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, weighted 10/25/35/30, skipping the last five days.
  • Trend, 40%. Share of the 20-, 50-, 100- and 200-day averages the price is above.

03Sizing and risk

15% annual volatility target, at most 40% in one fund. Rebalanced weekly. Risk-on: top four, 95% invested. Cautious: top two at 50%, the rest in bonds, gold and cash. Risk-off: bonds 20–40%, gold 15–30%, the rest cash.

04Results on record

The backtest file on disk runs from 2015-01 to 2026-03. Over that period the strategy returns 6.7% a year at 11.0% volatility, a Sharpe ratio of 0.61 with no risk-free rate deducted, and its deepest month-end drawdown is 13.3%. Costs of 5 basis points per trade are included.

05What works, and what does not

  • Works: the highest return of the four versions.
  • Limit: also the most volatile, so its Sharpe ratio is the lowest of the three that made money.
  • Concentration: four positions means one bad fund matters.
