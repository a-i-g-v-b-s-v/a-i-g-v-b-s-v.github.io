A.R.I.S

A systematic macro overlay that trades commodity futures from a four-factor signal conditioned on the growth and inflation regime.

A.R.I.S reads the macro regime from growth and inflation data, ranks 22 commodity futures on momentum, carry, value and reversal, sizes each position to a volatility target and sends the orders to Interactive Brokers behind four hard-coded risk checks.
