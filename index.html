TradingApr 2026 – Oct 2026

A.R.I.S

A systematic macro overlay that trades commodity futures from a four-factor signal conditioned on the growth and inflation regime.

A.R.I.S reads the macro regime from growth and inflation data, ranks 22 commodity futures on momentum, carry, value and reversal, sizes each position to a volatility target and sends the orders to Interactive Brokers behind four hard-coded risk checks. I ran it from April to October 2026 and then closed it. This page shows what it does, what its rules returned over those months, and where its results fall short of the coursework that inspired it.

What comes next

This site is the home for what I build. A.R.I.S, at the top of the selected work, is the centre of it: a macro view written as code and run against a broker under limits it cannot override.

The other projects feed it. The strategy book and the regime research supply ideas, the machine learning work tests them, and J.E.F.F.R.E.Y and the briefing keep me informed while it all runs. The nitro car is where the habit of taking things apart started. Each is described with its numbers and its limits, including the results that were poor.
