Who I am

Professional summary

I am finishing a Master's in Quantitative Finance at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business in December 2026. I trade, and I build quantitative models and the software around them. What I love is selling complex products and being around people: I run go-to-market part-time for Wildcat Labs, where the work brought in $300K of new annual recurring revenue in 60 days.

I am at my best where a product is technical and the buyer needs it explained properly. I ask questions, work out how the other person thinks, and position the product so that it makes sense to them. Having built strategies, risk limits and data pipelines myself, I can talk to the people who use such products in their own terms.

The areas I know best and want to work in are market infrastructure, compute infrastructure and energy. I am incredibly hungry and ambitious, competitive, and comfortable with risk and with fast-moving environments. I speak Portuguese, English, German and Spanish, and some French.

On the markets side, I worked on a fixed income desk at UBP in Geneva, where I built a sovereign spread model the desk adopted and pitched a trade that went into the live fund. At Plintus Capital Management I do quantitative research. Several projects on this site, among them A.R.I.S, ARIS500, J.E.F.F.R.E.Y and the daily briefing, began as tools I built for my own use.
